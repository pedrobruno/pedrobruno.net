# Wolfram.com

I still don't get all the hype about [Tweeter](http://www.tweeter.com) (??!?) ... but this thing, that's another story.

Developed by [Stephen Wolfram](http://www.stephenwolfram.com/about-sw/), it's one of the first practical approaches to a new way to present data. I wouldn't start selling any Google shares yet, but it looks very promising: [http://www45.wolframalpha.com](http://www45.wolframalpha.com)
